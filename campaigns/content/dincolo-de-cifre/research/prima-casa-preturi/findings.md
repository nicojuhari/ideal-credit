# Research — Did Prima Casă push up apartment prices?

Gate 2 (Research), 2026-10-29. Topic approved 2026-10-29.

## The program

- Prima Casă launched 2018 (HG nr. 202 din 28.02.2018; Legea nr. 70/2020). Original:
  loan up to 1M lei, 10% down payment, state guarantee 50%, age ≤45, employees only. (ODA)
- Prima Casă Plus: voted 31.07.2024, launched August 2024. Cap 1M -> 2.5M lei, term
  up to 30 years, open to non-employees with official income, guarantee 50% Chișinău /
  65% Chișinău homes ≤10 yrs old / 70% outside Chișinău (ODA; mded says "65% sau 70%").
  (mded.gov.md 31.07.2024; oda.md Prima Casă Plus page)
- **Max loan-to-value: 100% for Prima Casă vs 80% for every other mortgage** — BNM
  Q2 2026 Financial Stability Report, LTV chart ("Limita – 80%", "Limita „Prima Casă” – 100%").
  NewsMaker 08.05.2026: 2024 surge followed "Prima Casă ... fără avans".
- Rate H2 2026: max 8.66% (reference 5.66% + bank margin ≤3%) + 0.4% guarantee fee. (ODA)
- First 3 months of Plus: 1,000 families, 1.2bn lei loans, 720M lei guarantees, 90% urban,
  90% apartments, avg age 35. (ODA, ~26.11.2024)
- 2025: ~2,000 families, ~2.5bn lei (secondary: real-imobil.md citing Government — VERIFY)
- Outstanding state guarantees for Prima Casă end-2025: 4.5bn lei (Court of Accounts via
  bancamea.md 16.06.2026). Stock, NOT cumulative issuance.
- 01.04.2026: Finance Ministry stopped new **interest-compensation** requests — annual
  budget used up in Q1 by "volum foarte mare de cereri". Guarantees continue. Minister
  Gavriliță (02.05.2026): "Cu siguranță nu va rămâne așa cum este." No post-May decision
  found. (unimedia.info, moldpres.md, mf.gov.md)

## Prices (offer prices, Chișinău)

- BNM RPPI aggregate (2019 = 100): IV/22 ~136, IV/23 ~142, Q1 2025 211.7, Q2 2026 230.5
  (-1.1% q/q, +3.8% y/y). Primary 242.6, secondary 222.2. (BNM FSR Q2 2026, published
  23.09.2026)
- Q1 2025 jump: RPPI +18.4% q/q, +35.4% y/y. (BNM via zugo.md 27.05.2025)
- €/m² offer: 705 (2020), 1,070 (2023), 1,540 (2024), 1,720 (2025). (IPRE table, BNM/Acces Imobil)
- Global Property Guide: 1,749 €/m² Q2 2026.
- All are asking prices. No transaction-price index exists (IPRE recommends one).

## What BNM said (Q1 2025)

- Price growth "datorat, în mare parte, accesului extins la finanțare în cadrul programului
  guvernamental „Prima Casă Plus”".
- Prima Casă Plus loans/quarter: 310 (Q3 2024) -> 922 (Q1 2025). Share of mortgage
  contracts: 11% -> 30.2% (Q4 2024) -> 34.9% (Q1 2025).
- Median Chișinău price ~€89k–104.5k; BNM: convergence toward the 2.5M lei cap
  (~€127.8k) could push prices further. **← the cap acts as a price anchor**
- "majorarea prețurilor la imobile a început să inhibe cererea".

## What IPRE says (Nota analitică 2/2026, S. Ghilețchi, 05.05.2026)

- Apartment sales national: 22,565 (2024) -> 10,832 (2025), -52%. Chișinău 16,141 -> 6,334.
  Q1 2026: 1,340 national vs 4,245 Q1 2025 (-68%). (Cadastru)
- **2024: of 33,924 residential sales, only 2,430 financed by bank mortgage = 7%.
  "creșterea din 2024 a fost în primul rând un fenomen al resurselor proprii"**
- Mortgage share of sales: 4.2% (2023) -> 14.9% (2025) -> 60.7% (Jan–Feb 2026), mostly
  because cash buyers left, not because lending rose ("cumpărătorul fără acces la credit a
  dispărut din piață").
- Why prices don't fall: sellers not under pressure (mortgage arrears 0.9%, record low),
  no investment alternative, loss aversion; few deals set the reference for 15,000+ listings.
- Supply: Chișinău building permits for apartment blocks 86 (2024) -> 33 (2025).
- RPPI 226.7 vs CPI 183.2 in Q4 2025: housing now +43pp above inflation, after lagging -24pp.
- Affordability: ~10 years of average gross salary (17,400 lei, 2026 forecast) for 60 m².
- IPRE barely mentions Prima Casă.

## DATA PROBLEM (must be handled in the article)

NewsMaker 08.05.2026, Cadastru reply: a home bought with Prima Casă appears **only in the
"Ipotecă" column, not in "vânzare-cumpărare"**; new-build sales sit in "Alte bunuri imobile"
(1,674 Q1 2026 vs 5,122 Q1 2025). So:
- the "sales collapsed 52–84%" figures undercount Prima Casă and new-build purchases;
- IPRE's 7% / 60.7% mortgage shares use a denominator that may exclude Prima Casă buys.
The fall is real (5 consecutive quarters) but its size is uncertain. Cadastru promised more
detailed stats from Q2 2026 — check ipcbi.gov.md/ro/rapoarte-statistice-0 before drafting.

## UPDATE 2026-10-29 (draft gate): the "collapse" was mostly a counting gap

Since late 2024 notaries authenticate sales online; those deals were missing from public
Cadastru reports until 2026 (ziar.md / Ioniță, 07.08.2026: "Era o iluzie a prăbușirii
pieței, dar de fapt noi urmăream doar tranzacțiile offline"). By end-2025 ~half of deals
were digital.
- Chișinău apartments (Cernomorcenco, Acces Imobil, bancamea.md 26.10.2026, completed
  apartments, excl. off-plan): 2024 16,141; 2025 14,342 (-11%); Jan–Sep 2026 9,225 vs
  11,090 (-16.8%); Q1/Q2/Q3 2026 2,529 / 2,964 / 3,732; 2026 forecast ~12,570.
- Ioniță (revised, incl. digital + off-plan): Q1 2026 ~4,200 vs ~4,800; Q2 2026 ~7,500
  (5,000 existing + 2,500 off-plan), highest since 2021; 2025 fall "circa 20%".
- So IPRE's 6,334 (2025), 623 (Q1 2026), "-52%/-68%" and the 60.7% mortgage share are
  built on offline-only counts. DO NOT USE. 2024 (16,141) is ~the same in both series,
  so IPRE's 2024 "7% with bank credit / 93% own money" still roughly holds.
- Real picture: sales down ~11–17%, not 50–80%. Prices ~flat at the top.

## Abroad

- UK Help to Buy (2013–2023): Carozzi, Hilber & Yu, Journal of Urban Economics vol. 139
  (2024): in Greater London (little room to build) prices rose, construction didn't, and the
  price rise exceeded the value of the subsidy; near the Welsh border (room to build) more
  homes were built, prices didn't move. Developers built smaller units, bunched just below
  the price cap, and their profits improved. Gov evaluation 2017: new-build premium ~19%.
- Poland "Bezpieczny Kredyt 2%" (2023): NBP warned beforehand it would raise prices and
  mainly help better-off buyers; Adamiczka & Pluta (2025, 7 cities): improved affordability
  for those eligible, too short to raise supply, contributed to sharp price rise, prices kept
  rising after it ended. NBP (via Forsal): 7 largest cities +19.2% Jul 2023–Jun 2024 vs
  +2.9% the year before; NBP Q2 2024 y/y: Warsaw +17.5%, 6 other capitals +18.8%.

## Proposed angle

Not "Prima Casă raised prices — yes/no". The honest answer is split, and that is the story:

1. 2024 boom was mostly cash (IPRE: 93% own money) — the program didn't start it.
2. But Prima Casă Plus changed **who can still buy** and **at what price**: 100% LTV, a 2.5M
   lei ceiling that BNM saw prices drift toward, and by 2026 the credit buyer is most of what's
   left of the market. Subsidised demand meets a supply that's shrinking (permits 86 -> 33).
3. Abroad the same pattern: where supply can't respond (London), help ends up in the price;
   where it can, it builds homes.
4. Why prices don't fall: sellers aren't forced, no alternative investment, few deals set the
   price. Plus: the official sales numbers can't even show how many buy with Prima Casă.

Uniqueness: IPRE (May 2026) covers why prices don't fall but not the program's role, not
the cap-as-anchor, not international evidence, not who gained. No Moldovan piece found
joining BNM's view with IPRE's numbers.
