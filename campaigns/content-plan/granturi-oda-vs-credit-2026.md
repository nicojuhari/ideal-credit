## Recommended Outline

**H1:** Granturi ODA toamna 2026: grant, credit sau amândouă? Cum decizi înainte să depui

**Intro (100–150 words):** The calls are opening; most owners ask "mă calific?"; the better question is "cum arată banii mei în următoarele 6 luni dacă depun". State that the article is a decision tool and that Ideal Credit does not give grants.

**H2: Calendarul apelurilor ODA, septembrie–noiembrie 2026**
Pull the programme names and dates from oda.md on publish day and cite the source with the date checked. Keep this section short and clearly dated so it can be updated.

**H2: Ce s-a schimbat în 2026: mai puține granturi, mai multe instrumente de credit**
- 313,8M MDL grants in 2026 vs 346,4M in 2025 (source: ziarulnational / ODA)
- ODA guarantees up to 80% on bank credits (source: oda.md Fondul de Garantare)
- Programul 373 extended to 31.12.2028, 7% MDL / 3% FX, bank-channel only (source: gov.md, mded.gov.md)
- What it means for a small owner: the state is nudging toward credit; the grant queue gets tighter

**H2: Cum funcționează un grant, din perspectiva banilor tăi**
- Contribuție proprie (state the typical share as a range and cite the specific call; do not invent a figure)
- Spend first, reimburse later: the investment is made from your money, the grant arrives after verification
- Timeline: application → evaluation → contract → investment → reimbursement, typically months (cite ODA where possible)
- The cash-flow gap this creates is the part nobody draws

**H2: Grant vs credit: tabelul de decizie**
| Criteriu | Grant | Credit |
Rows: cost (0 vs dobândă) · certitudine (competiție vs decizie în zile) · timp până la bani · control asupra investiției (eligibilitate strictă vs liber) · birocrație · ce se întâmplă dacă ratezi termenul · impact pe cash-flow în primele luni

**H2: Când are sens combinația**
- Credit for the own contribution or for the pre-financing window, repaid at reimbursement (free early repayment is the specific Ideal Credit proof point here)
- Credit for the part of the investment the grant does not cover (e.g. used equipment, working capital, categories excluded from the call)
- Worked, clearly illustrative example with round numbers: investment, grant share, own share, months to reimbursement, cost of a 6-month bridge at the site's calculator figures

**H2: Când grantul e alegerea corectă și creditul nu**
Honest section: if the investment is fully eligible, you have the own contribution in cash, and you can wait, apply for the grant and do not borrow.

**H2: Ce faci dacă nu te califici sau ai ratat apelul**
Next call, Programul 373 through a bank, FCA for agriculture (link), OCN credit for speed and flexibility. State plainly which cases fit an OCN and which do not.
