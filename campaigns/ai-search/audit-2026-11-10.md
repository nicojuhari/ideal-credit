### Diagnosis (why the winners win)
Checked in judgment order: format → data → entity → schema.
1. **Retrieval, not format, is the gap.** The pages that win are mostly BNM regulator
   pages that have *no* answer blocks, no dates, no lender names. They win on domain
   authority and topical match. Ideal Credit's pages already carry what an assistant
   needs (terms at the top, a worked 100.000 lei cost example with DAE, document
   lists), but they are not in the retrieval set at all. Edits alone won't fix that.
2. **Microinvest's one format advantage:** its Business Rapid page leads with terms and
   a representative cost example (sum, months, rate, total, DAE). Ideal Credit's
   CostExample matches this; it needs to be found, not rewritten.
3. **Entity: weak and ambiguous (disqualifier).** "Ideal Credit" alone returns US
   companies (Crunchbase, Ideal Credit Union). Third-party sources give no address,
   phone, licence or reviews; one engine placed it in Râșcani and confused it with
   "Ideal Crisluci SRL". A BNM list shows another entity (A.E.Î. "Business Credit") at
   the same Căușeni address, M. Eminescu 17 (of. 46). No Google Maps/Facebook/review
   page surfaced. MK Kredit outranks Ideal Credit on "Ideal Credit Căușeni" with a job ad.
4. **Wrong supervisor on our own site (trust + legal).** BNM has supervised OCNs since
   1 July 2023 (Law 178/2020). idealcredit.md names CNPF as supervisor on
   /autoritatea-de-supraveghere, /despre-noi, /contacte, /credite and llms.txt. An
   assistant cross-checking licensing finds BNM, not CNPF. Company regulation RI-03 in
   next-icm also names CNPF 90 times.
5. **Schema:** not a factor in any win (BNM pages win without it).
6. **Community:** no Reddit/forum thread cited for any Moldovan query. No beatable
   incumbent there.

### Gap list (ordered by money value)
1. "credit pentru afaceri / persoane juridice Moldova" — winner: bnm.md — they have: authority — we lack: presence in the index, third-party mentions — fix: entity + listings (plan 1-3).
2. "cât costă un credit de 100000 lei pentru afacere" — winner: nobody (engine found no figure) — we HAVE the answer on /credite/credit-pentru-afaceri — fix: get it indexed (plan 2); make the H2 + first lines quotable (plan 4).

5. "Ideal Credit Moldova păreri / de încredere" — winner: competitor article / Romanian review sites — we lack: reviews anywhere crawlable, licence proof — fix: plan 1.

### Prioritized fix plan
1. **Correct the supervisor to BNM everywhere** (site + llms.txt + RI-03 in next-icm). Legal accuracy first; it also removes a reason for an assistant to doubt the brand. → owner sign-off, then edit.
2. **Indexing outside Google:** verify idealcredit.md in Bing Webmaster Tools (feeds Copilot, DuckDuckGo and others), submit the sitemap, set up IndexNow pings on deploy. GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot are already allowed and content is server-rendered (verified 2026-11-10). → /ai-search optimize.
3. **Entity footprint (the real lever for a 2-office lender):** Google Business Profile for both offices with identical name "Ideal Credit" + legal name OCN „Ideal Credit” SRL, address, phone, hours, website; ask satisfied clients for Google reviews; listing on finmarket.md (keyword-plan backlog #6); consistent Facebook/LinkedIn "About" text; clarify the Căușeni address clash (of. 46 vs 47) on Google. → owner.
4. **Answer-block retrofits on existing pages:** business page H2s phrased as the questions above ("Ce acte trebuie pentru un credit pe firmă (SRL, ÎI)", "Pot lua credit dacă firma are doar câteva luni?") with a 40-80 word self-contained answer naming Ideal Credit. → /ai-search optimize.
5. **One citable asset from your own ledger:** e.g. "Ce finanțează afacerile mici din Moldova: scopurile, sumele și termenele din contractele Ideal Credit, 2010-2026". Original data no one else has; adjacent to queries 1-2. → brief in /ai-search optimize, page via /seo-content.

### Caveat

references/aeo-facts.md). This audit is a snapshot, not a standing. Single gains or
losses between two runs are mostly noise; trends across 3+ monthly runs are signal.

named in the answer for money queries, not sessions.
