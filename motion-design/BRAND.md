# Motion brand kit: Ideal Credit
Created 2026-10-07 · last updated 2026-10-07

## Identity
Ideal Credit (OCN, Moldova) · https://idealcredit.md · "Îți spunem dacă are sens." Honest pre-application
verdict on a credit, backed by 16 years of pattern-recognition (see `brand/strategy.md`) · Audience: Moldovan
entrepreneurs and individuals, plain-Romanian readers (reading level 10-15 years old).

## Colours
Pulled 1:1 from `app/globals.css` (".dc" scope). Full rules: `brand/creative-kit.md`.
- Base dark: `#0b0b0b` · surface `#141414` · hairline `#212121` / `#333333`
- Base light (Thursday / Tips lane): `#f7f7f7` · surface `#ffffff` · hairline `#e3e3e3` / `#d1d1d1`
- Ink: `#f7f7f7` on dark, `#0b0b0b` on light · text-2 `#aaaaaa` dark / `#585858` light · text-3 `#818181`
- **ACCENT: `#FF9A00`** (one per film). Small orange type on light: `#c46e00`. Anything on an orange fill uses `#0b0b0b`.
- Secondary (sparingly): proof green `#86A873` (light: `#455d38`), only as the square dot of an uppercase kicker.
- Banned: gradients, glows, shadows, rounded corners, any second accent, the old warm `#100E0C` palette, orange kicker dots.

## Logo
Main mark: `brand/ideal-credit-logo.svg` (round, dark `#222` disc, works on dark and light). **End card only**, never
boxed, never echoed by another curve. In-frame branding = the `idealcredit.md` mono wordmark in the panel header
(same as social posts). End-card lockup: mark + `idealcredit.md`.

## Type
Display/context: Archivo 400/600 · Accent word (one per headline, like the site's `<Accent>`): Instrument Serif
italic 400, orange, 1.06em, tracking 0 (e.g. "Credite pentru *succes.*") · Figures, labels, kickers, source: IBM Plex Mono 400/500 · files in `brand/fonts/`
(latin + latin-ext for ă â î ș ț) · `tabular-nums` everywhere · tight tracking on big figures (-0.03 to -0.05em) ·
Never: Inter, system fonts.

## Voice
Straight-talking, warm, locally rooted. Plain Romanian, "tu" register, one idea per sentence. Do: are sens, verdict,
16 ani, situația ta, real MDL numbers. Don't: corporate jargon, "soluții financiare", jokes about debt, "fără refuz" /
distressed framing, regulator-fine framing. No em/en dashes in on-screen copy. Language: Romanian.
Sources: `brand/voice-profile.md`, `brand/strategy.md`, `brand/social-media-strategy.md`.

## Formats
Default master: 1080×1350 (4:5), 60 fps · Also deliver: 1080×1920 (9:16), panel recomposed into the centre, text
kept inside the centre 1080×1350 · Safe margin 72 px outer (social template) · Default duration: 6-10 s loops for posts.

## Sound
Muted-first: the type carries the message. Soft SFX (ticks, a single soft hit on the reveal); an optional calm
bed 90-110 BPM. Loudness -14 LUFS, TP -1. Used so far: see INDEX.md.

## Truth & assets
Every number comes from `films/<name>/facts.md` (source + date). Own calculations are labelled "calcul propriu …
exemplu ilustrativ" in the frame. Real UI: https://idealcredit.md (public, no login). Never show: client names, real
loan decisions, competitor names, "fără refuz", invented rates.

## Reference styles
The "Panou de date" social template (`campaigns/social/templates/`): flat panel, hairlines, kicker + wordmark header,
source cell at the base. Motion: calm financial desk, Stripe-docs precision, one thing moves at a time.
Past films: see INDEX.md.
