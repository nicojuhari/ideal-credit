# Keyword Plan - Ideal Credit

## Last Updated

2026-09-11 by /keyword-research (full rebuild - replaces 2026-05-18 plan; prior version archived at `campaigns/keywords/keyword-plan-2026-05-18.md`)

## Business Context

- Offer: Non-bank credits (OCN), 10,000-300,000 MDL, processed at offices in Căușeni and Chișinău
- Allocation directive (2026-09-11): **70-80% business credit content, 20-30% personal credit content** - personal scoped to planned purchases only (auto, reparație/renovare, nevoi personale majore), never distressed-borrower framing
- Audience: Moldovan entrepreneurs and small business owners (primary, 70-80% of content); individuals financing a planned purchase or project - car, home repair, a major personal expense (secondary, 20-30% of content)
- Goal: Organic leads matching the lender's actual target client - not the traffic the site currently attracts (see "Current Traffic Reality" below)
- Positioning: "The Local Growth Partner" / financial problem-solver first, credit provider second - fast capital + human judgment + local offices, never "instant cash, no questions asked"
- Language: Romanian primary; Russian secondary for Chișinău segment
- **Explicit exclusion (owner decision, 2026-09-06):** do not target distressed-borrower queries - "fără refuz," "istoric negativ," "fără loc de muncă," "restanțieri," "fără verificare," "credit până la salariu," "refinanțare" as a content topic. See "Excluded Segment" section for the data behind this call.

### Current Traffic Reality (why this plan is a repositioning bet, not a demand-chasing plan)

The Search Console export tells an uncomfortable but important story:

| Segment                                              | Clicks (6mo) | Share     | Example queries                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distressed-borrower (excluded)                       | 2,231        | 42%       | "credit online fara refuz" (545 clicks), "credite cu istoric negativ" (230), "credite fara verificare" (149), "credite nebancare pentru restantieri" (87) |
| Generic/branded "credit nebancar"                    | ~2,338       | 44%       | "credite nebancare" (327), "ideal credit" (299), "credite" (222), "credit nebancar" (110)                                                                 |
| Refinanțare (existing product, not a content target) | 387          | 7%        | "refinantare credit cu istoric negativ" (117), "refinantare credit" (81)                                                                                  |
| Personal credit (planned purchase)                   | 36           | 0.7%      | across auto/reparație/nevoi personale/bugetari terms                                                                                                      |
| **Business credit**                                  | **4**        | **0.07%** | 12 business-adjacent query rows total                                                                                                                     |

Business credit currently earns almost no organic traffic on this site. That is expected - this plan is a deliberate bet to reposition Ideal Credit as a business/entrepreneur lender, not a data-validated response to existing search demand. The business pillars below are validated against the _external_ Moldovan search market (competitor SERPs, BNM, government sources), not against idealcredit.md's own historical rankings, because the site has none yet in this category. Treat business-pillar "search volume" evidence as market-level, and treat the 90-day calendar as planting flags, not harvesting existing traffic.

### Excluded Segment - Do Not Target

Per owner decision (2026-09-06), the following are **explicitly out of scope** for SEO, blog, and content work, despite representing the largest share of current organic clicks:

- "fără refuz" / "credit fara refuz" (835 combined clicks across variants)
- "istoric negativ" / "credite cu istoric negativ" (230+ clicks)
- "fără verificare" (149 clicks)
- "fără loc de muncă" / "fara lucru oficial" (92+58 clicks)
- "restanțieri" (87 clicks)
- "credit până la salariu" (product being discontinued - do not create supporting content)
- "refinanțare" as a content topic **and as a standalone product page** (owner decision, 2026-09-11 round 3: the dedicated `/credite/refinantare` page is retired - it was generating bad-quality applications from people just moving debt between lenders with no concrete goal, per owner: "same clients with bad history, just moving debts, no concrete goal." Refinanțare/consolidare now exists only as a subservice section on `credit-pentru-nevoi-personale` and the business pages - see `site-architecture.md`)

**Why:** This traffic does not match Ideal Credit's positioning as a financial problem-solver for people with a real goal (grow a business, finish a renovation, buy a car) - it matches instant-cash-no-questions lenders like Mogo, Credit365, OK Credit. Chasing it would pull the brand back toward the microloan positioning it is actively moving away from. See [[project_seo_direction_2026-09]].

**Known copy leak to fix (not a content task, a technical/copy fix):** the homepage ranks ~position 6 for "fără refuz" terms. This should be treated as unintentional keyword contamination, not a win to build on.

## Pillar Overview - Personal (Target: 20-30% of content output)

**New pillar, added 2026-09-11.** The May 2026 plan had zero personal-credit content despite the stated 70/30 allocation - this closes that gap. Scope is deliberately narrow: only planned-purchase personal credit (auto, reparație/renovare, a defined major personal expense), never "fără verificare" instant-cash framing. Existing product pages already live: `credit-pentru-automobil`, `credit-pentru-nevoi-personale`, `credit-pentru-reparatie`, `credit-pentru-bugetari`.

### Pillar 6: Credit Personal pentru Proiecte Planificate - Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH

**Validation:**

- Search volume test: PASS (market-level) - "credit auto Moldova" and "credit reparatie casa Moldova" are active categories with real competitors (Mogo, Microinvest, MICB, EuroCreditBank); "credit bugetari" market-level search interest looks weak - mostly a Romanian (not Moldovan) term, and the existing `credit-pentru-bugetari` page earns only 5 clicks/118 impressions on GSC. **Recommend folding "bugetari" into the "nevoi personale" cluster rather than treating it as a standalone content target.**
- Market-centric test: PASS - but requires careful framing. "Doar cu buletinul," "fără verificare," "fără adeverință salariu" dominate the nevoi-personale SERP (Credit365, Mogo, OK Credit, eCredit) - this is exactly the distressed-adjacent tone Ideal Credit must avoid. The content angle is "planned project, real goal" (a car, a finished renovation, a defined expense) - not "fast cash, no questions."
- Competitive test: PASS - auto and reparație have real competitor content (Microinvest, EuroCreditBank, MICB) but it's product-page-only, no education/guide content; nevoi personale content is saturated but entirely instant-cash-toned, leaving the "planned expense" angle open
- Proprietary advantage: PARTIAL - no unique data here, but the "financial problem-solver first" positioning and human-judgment underwriting differentiate from the fully-automated competitors
