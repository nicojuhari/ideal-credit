# Keyword Plan - Ideal Credit

## Last Updated

2026-09-06 by /keyword-research (full rebuild; previous plan archived at `campaigns/keywords/keyword-plan-2026-05-18.md`)

## Business Context

- Offer: Non-bank credit organisation (OCN). Business credit is the core (70–80%): SRL, ÎI, GȚ, 20.000–500.000 MDL, from 3–6 months of demonstrable activity, no business plan and no profit required, fixed rate with decreasing instalments, free early repayment, decision in 1–2 working days. Personal credit is the rest (20–30%): planned purchases only - renovation, car, public-sector employees, personal needs.
- Audience: Moldovan small-business owners and serious, business-minded individuals who borrow with a clear goal.
- Explicitly out of scope (owner decision, 2026-09-06): the distressed-borrower segment - "fără refuz", "istoric negativ", "restanțieri", "fără loc de muncă", "fără verificare", "pînă la salariu". Refinanțare stays a product page but is not a content target: in practice those searchers are the same distressed profile.
- Goal: Organic leads with intent that matches the business - entrepreneurs evaluating financing, and individuals with a planned purchase.
- Positioning: "The Local Growth Partner" - financial problem-solver first, credit provider second. Content helps the reader decide correctly even when the answer is "not us" or "not now".
- Language: Romanian.

- Web searches performed: 30

- Competitor pages reviewed: Fagura (7 articles via SERP; site blocks fetch), Microinvest (blog listing, 9 posts), Prima Finanțare (full site), Corporația de Finanțare, Prime Capital, MicroÎmprumut, BNM, ODA, MAIB

### Research Mode

Data quality: LIVE. Search Console figures are real clicks and impressions. SERP observations are from live search on 2026-09-06. Two clusters marked `~` were not SERP-checked and are estimated.

### Key Discoveries

1. **The organic audience does not match the business.** Of 5.260 clicks, roughly 3.500 came from distressed-borrower queries ("credit online fără refuz" alone: 527 clicks, 4.976 impressions; "credite cu istoric negativ md": 224 clicks). Business queries brought 11 clicks on 696 impressions. The home page ranks around position 6 for "fără refuz" terms, which explains the 6.5% CTR and the traffic that does not convert. This is a positioning leak in existing page copy, not a content task - flagged, not targeted.

2. **The three May articles are live and indexed but invisible.** Ghid OCN: position 6.9, 353 impressions, 5 clicks. OCN vs bancă: position 8.1, 49 impressions, 0 clicks. Documente: position 23, 18 impressions. Business-credit search volume in Moldova is thin, so ranking well is not enough - each piece needs a sharper, decision-stage angle and internal links from the product pages.

3. **Fagura is now the only competitor publishing SME finance explainers**, and it published "Credit bancar vs finanțare alternativă Fagura" on 2026-05-22, a direct competitor to the OCN vs bancă article. Microinvest's blog (last 9 posts) is success stories, fraud awareness and funding-partnership news - no credit guides. Prima Finanțare has 11 offices including Căușeni and zero content. Nobody covers: credit on the company vs on the person, how "fără gaj" actually works (fidejusor), grant-plus-credit mechanics, FCA for farmers who do not qualify.

4. **Autumn 2026 is a grant season.** ODA opens new SME calls September–November 2026, with a strategic shift from grants (313,8M MDL in 2026 vs 346,4M in 2025) to guarantees and credit instruments. A grant-vs-credit decision piece published in September has a live news hook.

5. **The agricultural credit facility (FCA) is a large untouched explainer.** 5,1% fixed, up to 30% grant, only through banks, only for new equipment. The agriculture product page is the site's best-ranking product page (position 7). An honest explainer that says who qualifies, and what the alternative is when you do not, fits the positioning exactly.

6. **Personal traffic that does fit the business is small but converts.** Credit pentru reparație: position 8, 8,4% CTR (best on the site). Credit auto: 6.169 impressions, position 24, 0,8% CTR - the SERP is owned by car-loan specialists (Mogo, Primero, Autoplaza) who all require the car as collateral. An article about buying from a private seller or 999.md with a credit that is not secured on the car is the only unseen angle.

7. **Local Căușeni content has no search demand.** Zero Căușeni queries in six months; the only regional queries are Bălți (69 impressions total). The Căușeni landing page from the May plan is demoted to backlog.

8. **"Credite persoane juridice" gets impressions with no page.** 240 + 134 + 173 impressions across three variants, all around position 30. The business hub page should carry the term.

## Competitive Landscape

### Fagura

- Content: the only active SME-finance publisher. 2026 pieces: finanțare afacere la început (30.03), granturi IMM (20.02), cashback programme (14.04), credit bancar vs finanțare alternativă (22.05), microfinanțare cu grant (27.01)
- Gap: fully digital, no human-judgement angle, no company-vs-person, no fidejusor mechanics, no agriculture, no post-rejection guidance
- Threat: their bank-vs-alternative article competes directly with OCN vs bancă; refresh ours

### Microinvest

- Content: blog is success stories, fraud awareness, funding partnership news (BERD, CEB, EFSE, GGF). Product: up to 4–6M MDL fără gaj
- Gap: no decision guides, no cost explainers, no agriculture programme explainers despite AFD visits

### Prima Finanțare

- 11 offices including Căușeni. 20–31% fixed, up to 600.000 MDL, one fidejusor per 500.000 MDL, offers "pe firmă sau pe persoană fizică"
- Gap: zero content. Their own product option (company or person) is the topic of our DO FIRST article

### Corporația de Finanțare, Prime Capital, MicroÎmprumut

- Product pages only. Corporația: 500.000 MDL fără gaj, 17% fixed, 2% fee. MicroÎmprumut: credit line 50.000–3M, business refinanțare
- Gap: no editorial content at all

### BNM, ODA, MAIB

- Authoritative programme pages (Cum să solicitați un împrumut; Programul 373 extended to 2028, 7% MDL / 3% FX; FCA 5,1%; ODA guarantee up to 80%)
- Gap: dry, programme-centric, silent on who does not qualify
