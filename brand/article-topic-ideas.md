# Article Topic Ideas — Dincolo de Cifre

Backlog of pitched topics, checked against the Topic Gate in
`blog-editorial-strategy.md`. One line status per idea: `idea` → `in progress`
→ `published` (with slug + date once live). Pitched in batches of 4 on
2026-09-17; pick one at a time per the "one topic at a time" workflow rule.

## Backlog

- **Buy, Borrow, Die** — draft ready for review (slug `cumpara-imprumuta-mori`, 2026-09-29). Angle: the "borrow" step is mostly myth (Fox & Liscow 2026: "buy, save, die"); Moldova has the same step-up + untaxed inheritance. How billionaires (Musk, Bezos, Ellison) pay
  close to $0 tax for decades: never sell stock, borrow against it instead,
  die, heirs inherit at stepped-up basis, tax on the gain never happens.
  Global, mechanism-driven, "wait, that's legal?" effect. Source: ProPublica's
  2021 tax-return investigation.

- **The eurodollar system** — idea. Trillions of "US dollars" exist and get
  lent bank-to-bank entirely outside the US banking system and Fed oversight.
  Bigger, more abstract wow-factor, skews toward a finance-literate reader
  more than the others.

- **Did Prima Casă raise apartment prices?** — idea (pitched 2026-10-02).
  State-guaranteed mortgages turned 30-40% of purchases into mortgage buys;
  Chișinău prices reached ~€1,720/m² in H1 2025 and stayed there even though
  sales fell ~54% (9M 2025 vs 2024). Mechanism: subsidised demand ends up in
  the price; prices don't fall easily. Global comparison: UK Help to Buy
  studies. Sources: Global Property Guide, Accesimobil, Logos Press — needs
  official data (BNM, ODA) before drafting.

- **Gold now outweighs the euro in central-bank reserves** — idea (pitched
  2026-10-02). End-2025: gold 27% of global reserves vs euro 15%, Treasurys
  22% — mostly because the price rose, not because banks bought more
  (contrarian frame). Record 288.9 t bought in Q2 2026. Moldova angle: how much
  gold BNM holds and why (one report says ~€9m of ~€5.4bn reserves — VERIFY,
  may be a misreading). Sources: WGC, IMF COFER.

- **How much does Moldova still live on remittances?** — idea (pitched
  2026-10-02). Remittances ~$1.9bn in 2025, flat vs 2024. Old "a quarter of
  GDP" figure is likely outdated as the economy grew — check the real current
  share vs the ~2006 peak. Questions: how big, where the money goes
  (consumption vs housing vs firms), what happens as emigrants settle abroad.
  Sources: BNM balance of payments, World Bank — VERIFY share before pitching
  the headline.

## Published

- **SEPA, one year on** — slug: `sepa-transfer-euro-moldova`, published
  2026-10-02 with BNM's 10-month data (owner chose not to wait for the
  one-year figures). Angle: SEPA is ~80% of euro transfers by count but ~46%
  by value; €13.2m saved is ~0.09% of flows, so small transfers (diaspora,
  small firms) gain most. Big payments stay on SWIFT because China, Ukraine,
  Turkey (~31% of imports) aren't in SEPA. Refresh when BNM publishes the
  full-year numbers.

  timely peg: BNM base rate 7.5% -> 9% on 2026-09-17, inflation 6.96% (Aug).
