## Category
Non-bank business credit (OCN) in Moldova for micro/small firms. Ticket size
10.000-500.000 lei, plus a personal-credit line. Banks (maib) compete directly for
the same client.

Roster logic: Microinvest = leader. MK Kredit, Prima Finanțare, Easy Credit = OCN
peers. maib = the bank going after OCN business clients. Fagura = digital up-and-comer.

## Positioning map

**Saturated (3+ say it, dead territory):**
- Speed. "Decizia în 2 ore" / "Decizia în maxim 24 ore" (Microinvest), "1 oră" (maib
  Microbusiness), "în maxim 1 oră de la aprobare" (Easy Credit), "de la o oră până la
  24 de ore" (MK Kredit FAQ), "răspuns în maximum 24 de ore" (Fagura).
- Fără gaj. Microinvest "Până la 6.000.000 lei fără gaj", maib "500 000 lei... fără gaj",
  Prima "600.000 MDL fără gaj", Easy Credit credit line "fără gaj", Fagura "nu este
  necesar să depui un gaj".
- No paperwork. Microinvest "Nu este nevoie de dosare, business plan sau copii ale
  documentelor contabile", Fagura "fără birocrație", maib "cererea online durează 3
  minute". Ideal Credit's own pages also say "Fără plan de afaceri" (same claim).
- Fixed rate. Microinvest "Dobânda este fixă pe toată perioada", Prima "Dobândă fixă
  pe toată perioada creditului", Ideal Credit "dobândă fixă".
- CTA "Aplică" / "Aplică online" / "Aplică acum" (Microinvest, MK, Prima, maib).
- Calculator on every product page (Microinvest, MK, Prima, Fagura, Ideal Credit).
- Digital/app. Microinvest app business requests (21 Sep 2026), Easy Credit eCredit
  app, maib "aprobare 100% online", Fagura "100% online în doar 15 minute".

**Contested (2 say it, fight or flank):**
- Help preparing the file / business support. maib Business Voucher: "consultanță
  gratuită... elaborarea planului de afaceri; pregătirea documentației pentru dosarul
  de credit", 500-1.000 EUR, "disponibile până în noiembrie 2026". Microinvest Business
  Growth Program (EBRD): digitalisation, AI and marketing courses, Jun-Dec 2026. Both
  are donor-funded and time-boxed, and neither tells the client *whether* to borrow.
- Start-ups and young firms. Prima "Prima alegere în finanțarea Start-Up-urilor și
  Antreprenorilor"; maib business card "până la 40.000 MDL" for firms with "mai puțin
  de 12 luni". Ideal Credit FAQ: "Am finanțat firme cu 4-6 luni de activitate".
- Longevity. Microinvest "De peste 23 de ani"; Easy Credit founded 2005. Ideal Credit
  has 16 years (third, not a lead).
- Refinancing other lenders. maib Refinanțare Plus "Ai luat în grabă un credit pentru
  afacerea ta?"; Easy Credit lists refinancing. MK charges "3,0% - in cazul in care
  creditul este refinantat de alte institutii". Owner retired refinanțare (2026-09-11),
  so this is not our fight.

**White space (no one says it):**
- Whether this credit will work for the goal, and how much is the right amount. All
  six sell access; none sells the answer. Demand evidence: «не у всех предпринимателей
  есть необходимые навыки для получения финансирования» (SME Alliance, newsmaker.md,
  2026-03-26). maib paying donor money for file-prep consultancy shows the need exists.
- When the bank or a state program is the better choice. maib lists FACEM "5,5 %",
  Programul „373" "7%", FCA "5,1%" but only as its own products. No OCN mentions any
  cheaper route.
- A named business-size cost example on an OCN business page. Only Microinvest shows
  one (500.000 lei / 12 luni / DAE 25,46%). MK hides rates in a scanned PDF, maib in a
  tariff PDF, Easy Credit and Prima show only consumer examples or a range.

## Pricing landscape

Business-credit DAE floor about 24-25% (Microinvest, MK Business/Agro). Ceiling:
**Ideal Credit about 60%** (site calculator at 4%/month gives DAE 60,16-60,53% for
10.000-300.000 lei, computed 2026-11-10). Consumer DAE clusters at 45-60% everywhere.

Read: big lenders price business credit at about 25% DAE and publish it. The
mid-tier (MK, maib, Easy Credit) hide the business rate. Prima and Fagura discount
through raffles and promos (Prima "TOMBOLA DE CRĂCIUN", Fagura "Primul tău credit de
1.000€ cu dobândă 0%"). That signals demand softness at the small end. Ideal Credit is
priced like a consumer OCN in a business market. That can't be hidden, so it has to be
explained (gap 2). AI summaries already label Ideal Credit "o opțiune scumpă"
(keyword-plan.md).

## The 3 exploitable gaps

1. **"Is this credit right for your goal, and how much?" Nobody answers it.**
   Evidence: the speed lenders sell the absence of analysis: Microinvest "Nu este nevoie
   de dosare, business plan", maib "1 oră", Easy Credit "în maxim 1 oră", MK "de la o
   oră". The only help in the market is file prep (maib voucher, donor-funded, ends Nov
   2026) or marketing training (Microinvest BGP). Fagura blogs "Cum calculezi nevoia
   reală de finanțare" but decides by "Scoring". Demand: the SME Alliance quote above;
   maib needed a donor program to fill the gap.
   Why they can't close it: their unit economics are automated decisions in hours.
   Advising on the amount, or against the loan, slows the funnel and cuts volume they
   are funded to originate (EBRD/BSTDB/EFSE lines).
   Fix first: our own pages say "Fără plan de afaceri". That is the competitors' claim,
   and it hides the mechanism. Show *what* we look at instead, e.g. extrase bancare and
   the goal.
   Exploit via /direct-response-copy on the business pages (the mechanism + one real
   "smaller amount / different structure" case), then /social-content for the Tue
   success lane.

2. **REJECTED by owner 2026-11-10: never send clients to banks; answer strong clients with a lower rate instead.** Kept for the record. "When the bank or a state program is the better choice", and why we cost more.
   Evidence: Ideal Credit's DAE (about 60%) is the category ceiling vs Microinvest
   about 25% and MK ≤24-32%. AI summaries already call us "o opțiune scumpă". maib
   publishes 5,1-7% state-program rates but only sells its own products. No OCN names
   a cheaper route. Search citations for business credit come from BNM and funding
   news, not from any lender comparison page.
   Why they can't close it: an OCN pointing clients to ODA/373/FCA gives away its own
   loan, and banks won't map who they reject. Only a lender whose strategy is the honest
   verdict (strategy.md) can say "go to maib for this one" without breaking its model.
   It turns the price weakness into the proof.
   Exploit via /seo-content (keyword-plan backlog #9 "Credit nebancar sau bancar: când
   are sens pentru firma ta", blocked on owner sign-off for cost wording) + /ai-search
   so it becomes the cited answer.

3. **Young and thin-file firms (ÎI, GȚ, 4-12 months, no annual report).**
   Evidence: Microinvest needs an SRL and "o istorie creditară pozitivă". MK needs the
   last year's financial report. maib limits imply "minimum 12 luni de activitate".
   Fagura wants a year (RO guide). Ideal Credit FAQ: "Am finanțat firme cu 4-6 luni de
   activitate cu flux demonstrabil", SRL/ÎI/GȚ, judged on extrase bancare.
   Contested by maib Microbusiness (ÎI, ≤500k, "1 oră") and Prima (start-ups, small,
   invisible in search).
   Why they can't close it easily: scoring models need statements and bureau history.
   Manual review of bank-account flow doesn't scale for them.
   Exploit via /seo-content as a service/solution page (credit pentru ÎI / firmă nouă),
   plus a line in the business-page hero. Verify search demand in GSC first.

Not used as gaps (by owner rule, lead positive): Easy Credit's CNPF breaches, the MK
BNM fine, the Fagura cyberattack. Context only, not copy.
