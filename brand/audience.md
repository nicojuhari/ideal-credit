## Segments

### 1. Farmers and agro firms (GȚ, ÎI, SRL): best evidenced, 16 quotes
- Who: field-crop and fruit growers, often in the south (Cahul, Ștefan Vodă), already
  carrying bank, OCN and supplier debt (VOC-P03, VOC-P09, VOC-S03).
- Job to be done: get cash across the gap between a bad season, late subsidies and the
  next harvest without losing the machinery or the house (VOC-P02, VOC-P06, VOC-P07).
- Awareness: **solution-aware.** They name the fixes themselves: "refinanțare",
  "reeșalonare", "long-term crediting", a moratorium (VOC-D02, VOC-D03, VOC-P02).
- Emotion: distrust turning to anger at lenders who "ignorând ciclul agricol" (VOC-P01).

### 2. Micro and small non-agro firms (SRL/ÎI, services, trade, production): the 80% focus, provisional
- Evidence is thin online. These owners don't post about credit publicly. We have 4
  quotes: VOC-D05, VOC-O13, VOC-O14, VOC-P10. Plus the positioning.md priors.
- What the little there is says: they lead with "без залога" (VOC-D05). They accept a
  higher rate for a lender that is "simple" and a "business partner" (VOC-O13, VOC-O14).
  The SME Alliance says many lack the skills to get financed (positioning.md).
- Awareness: likely **solution-aware** (they compare bank vs OCN), not yet evidenced.
- **Gap: the next mine for this segment is the owner's own call notes and client
  conversations, not the web.**

### 3. Personal borrowers (the 20%)
- Who: wage earners, pensioners, people with income from abroad (VOC-P18, VOC-V01,
  VOC-O04, VOC-O15).
- Job to be done: an urgent purchase (car, phone) or a gap, without being "squeezed"
  (VOC-P18, VOC-O01).
- Awareness: **product-aware**. They compare banks with "kantore" by collateral and
  penalties (VOC-O02).
- Emotion: distrust (VOC-O01, VOC-O06, VOC-O07).

## Desires & Buying Triggers
- Being understood when things go wrong: "dialog și comunicare" (VOC-D01), "проявляли
  понимание" even when late (VOC-D04).
- No collateral: "без залога" (VOC-D05), "fara gaj" (VOC-O02).
- Long-term credit over grants (VOC-P02). Restructuring over enforcement (VOC-D02).
- A lender that is trustworthy before it is cheap: "o cantora buna si de incredere" (VOC-D07).
- Russian-language information (VOC-D06).

## Dominant Emotion & Sophistication
- **Farmers: distrust → anger** at being treated as a file, not a season (VOC-P01, VOC-S01, VOC-P02).
- **Personal: distrust** of small lenders as a trap (VOC-O01, VOC-O07, VOC-O06).
- **Business non-agro: unknown.** The positioning.md priors suggest uncertainty about
  their own readiness ("skills"), not anger.
- Sophistication: Stage 3-4. People shrug at "rapid" and "fără gaj" (every lender says
  it, see competitors.md) and react to proof of conduct: how the lender behaved when
  things went wrong (VOC-D04, VOC-P01).

## Surprises
1. **Pushback on purpose questions.** "deja treaba lui ce face cu banii, nu ?" (VOC-O05).
   Our mechanism, "analizăm scopul și situația financiară", can read as nosy to personal
   borrowers. Frame it as protecting them, not vetting them. Business owners may react
   differently; not evidenced.
2. **The loudest agro demand is refinancing**, the service the owner retired on
   2026-09-11 for lead quality (VOC-D02, VOC-D03). Don't reopen it. Do expect farmers to
   ask, and have a straight answer ready.
3. **Supplier credit is the real competitor for farm working capital**, not the bank
   (VOC-S03, VOC-P09). Seeds and inputs "pe datorie".
4. **How the lender behaves in a bad month is the proof people remember.** The best
   recent OCN review praises understanding during late payments (VOC-D04). The worst
   story is enforcement mid-harvest (VOC-S01). Neither is about the rate or the speed.
5. **The 80% segment is almost silent online.** Business-owner VoC barely exists in
   public Moldovan sources. The owner's call notes are worth more than another web pass.
6. **Russian-language demand shows up again** (VOC-D06, VOC-O08, VOC-O15). It matches
   the keyword-plan note that no lender has a Russian business-credit page.
